The Gould Creative site uses a warm cream paper background with an olive/sage brand green accent (`--accent: #6f7047`) and deep warm-ink text. This palette is sourced from the company overview deck, so it is genuine brand, not the AI-default beige+brass premium-consumer palette.

**Why:** A redesign pass could flag cream+olive+espresso as the banned premium-consumer AI palette and swap it. That would break brand recognition.

**How to apply:** Preserve the olive+cream identity. To add drama, use the dark warm-olive panel tokens (`--panel`, `--panel-ink`) as accent bands rather than changing the accent hue. Display headlines use Saira Semi Expanded (`.display`), chosen to match the company deck's wide geometric technical headline font; small labels stay mono-uppercase (`.eyebrow`). The deck's exact display font is a licensed Eurostile/"square"-genre face; Saira Semi Expanded is the closest free next/font match.
